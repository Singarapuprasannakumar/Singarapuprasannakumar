<summary><b>💬 MeetMind — Collaborative Discussion Platform</b></summary>

<br/>

> **Where teams think together, not just talk.**

MeetMind is a real-time collaborative discussion platform designed for structured team decision-making — turning chaotic conversations into organized, actionable outcomes.

| | |
|--|--|
| **Problem** | Teams lose context, decisions get buried in chat threads, and follow-ups fall through the cracks |
| **Solution** | Structured discussion workflows with real-time sync, threaded decisions, and persistent context |

**Key Features**
- `Real-Time Communication` — live discussion rooms with instant sync
- `Structured Workflows` — organized decision threads with clear ownership
- `Team Collaboration` — multi-user sessions with role-based participation
- `Decision Tracking` — persistent record of outcomes and action items

**Stack:** `React` `Node.js` `Express.js` `MongoDB` `REST APIs`

🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/Singarapuprasannakumar)
